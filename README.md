## ✨ Key Features

- 🎯 **Adjustable FOV** for engagement control
- 🌊 **Smoothing and speed curves** for natural travel
- 🫱 **Humanized movement** with jitter and reaction delay
- 🦴 **Hitbox priority** — head, chest, or nearest point
- 👁️ **Visibility check** for exposed targets
- 🔫 **Per-weapon profiles** for rifles, SMGs, and snipers
- 🌀 **Recoil control** with separate axes
- ⚡ **Triggerbot** with delay and burst
- ⌨️ **Hold and toggle** activation

## 🚀 Why Choose Crossfire Aimbot?

Maps are compact and fights are short, so a narrow FOV keeps the crosshair predictable. The tool separates FOV, smoothing, and stopping point, then adds per-weapon profiles for different site setups.

The visibility check keeps boxes and smoke from pulling the crosshair.

## 🎮 Why Use It?

- **Tune for close fights** with a narrow FOV
- **Build consistency** across weapons
- **Practice one-taps** with delay control
- **Handle eco rounds** with pistol profiles
- **Switch profiles** instantly

## 🧩 Quick Start

1. Launch **Crossfire** and open the panel.

3. Choose a hitbox priority and enable visibility checks.
4. Configure trigger delay and recoil strength.
5. Save the setup as a weapon profile.
